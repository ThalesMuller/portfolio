# Arte e fontes

A imagem decorativa foi gerada com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. As versões de publicação são [structure.webp](../public/art/structure.webp), com 1056 × 1056 pixels e 48 KB, e [social.jpg](../public/art/social.jpg), com 1200 × 630 pixels e 55 KB. A composição representa um objeto abstrato.

Instrument Sans e IBM Plex Mono são auto-hospedadas por pacotes Fontsource sob OFL. O PDF incorpora os arquivos Liberation Sans em `src/resume/fonts/` com a licença distribuída ao lado.

Create a single believable architectural sculpture: an isometric precision assembly of interlocking, thick rectangular open frames made of satin charcoal anodized aluminum and dark translucent teal glass. Three distinct layers offset with geometric purpose, one narrow pale spring-green edge or insert running through the structure as a restrained accent. Tangible machined faces, sharp bevels, clean diagonal perspective, subtle shadows on a dark matte work surface. Fine dim drafting grid only on the background surface, no markings or labels. Editorial product photography, art-directed, close three-quarter angle, balanced broadly square sculpture, dramatic but soft light from upper left, visibly three-dimensional, enough open air around the edges. Deep black-green near #0e2222 and teal-gray shadows, brightest highlights on metal and frosted glass, restrained yellow-green detail. The composition should feel quiet and sophisticated, like a photograph of a design object. Let the sculpture occupy 75% of width and height with comfortably dark edges on every side. No text, numbers, symbols, interface, logos, watermark, light streaks, people, or fantasy environment.
